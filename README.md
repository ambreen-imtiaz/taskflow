# ⚡ TaskFlow Pro

A modern, fast, and highly customizable personal productivity dashboard designed to help you organize tasks, manage priorities, and track daily goals with ease.

---

## 🌟 What is TaskFlow Pro?

**TaskFlow Pro** is an all-in-one task management workspace built to replace overly complicated productivity tools. Whether you need to manage daily personal errands, track team projects, or visualize work using an interactive board, TaskFlow Pro keeps your workflow organized, colorful, and completely private.

All your task data stays directly in your web browser, meaning your privacy is guaranteed and no account creation or login is ever required.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 📊 Executive Command Center
* **Live Productivity Clock:** Keeps real-time track of your work session alongside an urgent task reminder.
* **Smart Stat Cards:** Instant visual summaries of your total workload, high-priority tasks, active tasks, and completed items.
* **Goal & Streak Tracker:** A visual progress bar that updates automatically as you complete tasks.

### 📋 Flexible Views for Every Workflow
* **Interactive Kanban Board:** Drag and drop tasks seamlessly between *To Do*, *In Progress*, and *Completed* columns.
* **Task List View:** A clean, searchable list showing categories, priority tags, and due dates.
* **Calendar View:** Map out your upcoming dates to stay ahead of deadlines.
* **Productivity Hub:** Track your overall completion velocity and performance metrics.

### 🎨 Fully Customizable Color Themes
* **Instant Presets:** Switch between pre-made themes including *Indigo Cyber*, *Neon Pink*, *Emerald Dark*, *Warm Amber*, *Ocean Blue*, and *Deep Purple*.
* **Custom Color Pickers:** Fine-tune the primary accent, main background, and card colors to build your own personal setup.

### 📁 Data Privacy & Management
* **100% Local Storage:** Your data stays on your device—no external server access or data tracking.
* **Data Export:** Backup your task data anytime as a downloadable JSON file.
